A second gamepad can run the intake, launcher and fire while the first one drives, like gamepad2 on a Driver Station.

How to score

  1. Hold the intake and drive over Pollen (yellow). The robot holds at most 4 balls (G407).
  2. Face your upward Cell on the red Hive, press auto-aim, then hold fire.
  3. 8 Pollen in the upward Cell tips the Hive: 20 points, and the other Cell comes up.
  4. Score Flowers and the Garden as well. Your best score stays in this browser.

The robot is a placeholder (18 in / 0.46 m mecanum, 13 kg / 28.7 lb) until the real robot is measured. Physics, scoring and fouls come from the same simulator the team's AI trains in.
