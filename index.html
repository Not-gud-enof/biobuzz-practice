Headless: push the stick away from you and the robot drives away from you, whichever way it faces. With no turn input the robot holds its heading, so bumps don't spin it. A second gamepad can run the intake, launcher and fire while the first one drives, like gamepad2 on a Driver Station.

How to score

  1. Hold the intake and drive over Pollen (yellow). The robot holds at most 4 balls (G407).
  2. Drive to about 1 m (3 ft) from your upward Cell, on the side it opens toward. Turn on aim lock: the turret turns to the Cell and the flywheel sets its speed; you keep driving. Hold fire; it shoots only when locked on.
  3. Full match: in the 30 s AUTO, score your 4 pre-loaded Pollen and park in the Loading Zone (LEAVE 3 + TIP 20 + PARK 5). The robot stops for the 8 s transition, then TELEOP runs 2:00; park again at the end (5).
  4. 8 Pollen in the upward Cell tips the Hive: 20 points, and the other Cell comes up.
  5. Score Flowers and the Garden as well. Your best score stays in this browser.

The robot is a placeholder (18 in / 0.46 m mecanum, 13 kg / 28.7 lb) until the real robot is measured. Physics, scoring and fouls come from the same simulator the team's AI trains in.
